# Step back: one read across your sessions

**Sessions has a new first tab, Step back.** With 315 sessions open, the list
itself had become the problem. One click now reads what you said across the
sessions of the last 30 days (7, 14 or 60 if you prefer) and shows what you are
really after, where each thread drifted and whose move that was, what waits on
you, and at most five next moves. It asks three or four questions back and keeps
your answers for the next read. A Step back chip on World and Projects opens it.

**The certain part needs no model and shows at once**: open sessions, what waits
on you by kind (sign-ins, questions, not pushed, to review, unread), old sessions
that are finished, and how you steered: turns in your own words against turns
where you clicked the reply a session offered or started a side session it
proposed.

**The read is one sealed, read-only job** by Claude Code or Codex under your own
sign-in. It gets only your own messages, cut short, in a folder that is deleted
when it finishes; the page says so and waits for you before the first read.

**Plan Quest can hold the trail.** Each goal links to a plan. After one more
approval (work records only; plans and milestones stay read-only) Save to
PlanQuest leaves a short record per goal, answer and decision on its plan.

**Every session can see your goals.** A new MCP tool, `hangar_stepback`, gives
any Claude Code or Codex session the latest read: goals, your call on each, next
moves, open questions. A rule in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `~/.codex/AGENTS.md`
tells sessions of both agents to name the goal their work serves before sizeable
work or a side task, and to say so when a session has moved away from what it
began as.

## Key decisions

- Facts and the read are separate layers, so nothing certain depends on a model
  and the read is always labelled as the reader's.
- "Whose move" is measured, not guessed: the Claude app records who each turn
  came from, and a message that matches a reply the session had just offered was
  a click.
- Message text lives in its own store (`session-voice.json`), never in the
  session index, and only your side of the conversation.
- The reader runs with `--restricted --strict-mcp-config` and reading tools only:
  your messages are data to it, and it has no tool that could act on them.
- Plan Quest writes go through the console from the engine's saved read, never
  from text the browser sends, and ask for `records:write` only.
- Archiving stays your click, through the same path as Clean up.
- See `docs/step-back.md`.
